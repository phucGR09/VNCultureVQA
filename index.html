Overview

Abstract

Visual Question Answering (VQA) in low-resource languages remains largely disconnected from the cultural and contextual knowledge required to interpret real-world imagery. Existing Vietnamese VQA benchmarks primarily treat images as isolated inputs, limiting evaluation to visual recognition rather than knowledge-grounded multimodal reasoning. We introduce VNCultureVQA, a large-scale benchmark for Vietnamese cultural understanding that jointly grounds images and questions in real-world cultural articles, linking each image to its source article, human-authored caption, and contextual knowledge. VNCultureVQA contains 107,947 image–article pairs and 691,830 question–answer pairs spanning six cognitive levels, from visual recognition to global contextual synthesis.

The benchmark supports knowledge-grounded generative VQA under progressively enriched contextual settings, enabling evaluation of visual understanding and culturally grounded multimodal reasoning. We further establish three progressively enriched evaluation settings, ranging from image-only VQA to article-grounded VQA and supervised domain adaptation. Experiments with representative Vietnamese and multilingual vision-language models show consistent gains from contextual grounding, while consensus and lexical scores degrade steadily as reasoning demands increase and supervised domain adaptation yields uneven improvements, revealing persistent limitations in current multimodal models.

Dataset

Construction Pipeline

Each article is modelled as a unified multimodal knowledge source: full body text, embedded images and human-authored captions are kept together, so every question is grounded in the narrative its image came from.

Six-level cognitive taxonomy

Questions are organised into three meta-groups. Purely visual questions are deliberately capped at 8.2% to mitigate visual shortcuts and language priors; 92.0% of questions require multimodal integration or higher-level reasoning.

Visual-only questions are first detected with keyword and part-of-speech analysis and validated zero-shot with Qwen3-VL-8B. The remaining questions are scored with Chain-of-Thought prompting, and the final difficulty score is a weighted ensemble in which the higher Gemini weight reflects its stronger reasoning on Levels 5–6:

$$S_{\text{final}} = 0.7 \cdot S_{\text{Gem}} + 0.3 \cdot S_{\text{Qwen}}$$

Limitations

Discussion & limitations

Gemini-generated QA pairs carry risks of stylistic homogeneity and hallucination, and web-sourced content may partially inflate zero-shot performance through pretraining-corpus overlap — a contamination risk that InfoSeek addressed with Unseen-Entity splits not yet provided here. Monolingual coverage limits cross-lingual evaluation compared with SEA-VQA and CVQA.

On the societal side, the dataset supports culturally aware vision-language models and the digital preservation of Vietnamese heritage for education, though LLM-generated content may encode factual errors. Reliance on major publications introduces representational bias toward dominant cultural narratives, partially mitigated by detailed source documentation and human verification of the full test split.
